Customer churn dashboard

Who left, who stayed, how much monthly revenue walked out with them, and which segments to look at first. Use the filters to slice any number or chart on the page.

Filters

Retained Churned Bars show each group's share of customers (first three charts) or monthly revenue (revenue chart). Hover or focus a bar for exact counts.

Segments: plan × contract

Every combination of subscription plan and contract type in the current filter, with its churn rate and monthly revenue lost.

How each number is defined

Churn rate
Customers with Churn = Yes ÷ customers in the current filter.
Retention rate
1 − churn rate: customers still active ÷ customers in the current filter.
Revenue at risk
Monthly revenue (MRR) already lost: the sum of MonthlyCharges for churned customers, with the share of total MRR and the ×12 annual equivalent. As a forward-looking check it also shows MRR from active customers on Month-to-Month contracts, the group where churn is concentrated.
Tenure KPIs
Average TenureMonths overall, for churned customers and for retained customers. Tenure bands used by the filter: 0–6, 7–12, 13–24 and 25+ months.
Support tickets
Average SupportTickets per customer, churned vs retained.
Currency
The source file gives MonthlyCharges and TotalCharges without a currency, so revenue figures are shown as plain amounts.

Source: customer_churn_sample.csv (15 customers, 11 fields, no missing values). The sample is small, so a single customer moves a rate by about 7 points. Read the percentages as direction, not as forecasts. Contract type and tenure overlap in this data (every Month-to-Month customer has 14 months of tenure or less, every other customer has 15 or more), so the two effects cannot be separated here.
